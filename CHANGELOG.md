# Changelog

## v0.2.1 (2026-11-19)
- USGS Imagery basemap works again: USGS retired its imagery-with-labels service.

## v0.2.0 (2026-11-19)
- A reach's popup shows its cross-sections beside the reach medians.
- Drag or type a section's bankfull depth and low-bank height.
- An edited reach is screened on its edited sections; map and sites follow.
- Pull any section from USGS 3DEP on request, as the grid sampled it.
- Edits are saved with the screening (file version 2) and marked in downloads.
- Faster hover on the map; watershed names in the cue; examples open Screen.
- Third-party notices: geotiff and its packages added, fonts' notice completed.

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-19)
- First beta of Step 1, Screen sites, on the national cross-section grid.
- Pick watersheds, everything upstream of a point, or a box as the study area.
- Reaches of about 1,000 ft colored by bank height ratio or entrenchment ratio.
- Criteria with a funnel that shows how each one narrows the reaches.
- Candidate sites along each main stem, ranked, kept or dropped, with notes.
- Shortlist as CSV and GeoJSON, every reach as CSV, and a printable summary.
- Save and Open a screening; Steps 2 to 4 shown as planned.
