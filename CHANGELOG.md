# Changelog

## v0.3.1 (2026-11-20)
- Watersheds: pick by HUC12, HUC10 or HUC8, then click the map to add one.
- A whole HUC10 or HUC8 shows as one chip, in place of the Add whole buttons.

## v0.3.0 (2026-11-19)
- Reaches: click reaches, or hold Shift and drag boxes, to screen known sites.
- Draw a box is gone: a saved box opens as picked reaches.
- A shorter Study area step, without the examples.
- A simpler Screen step: three criteria with the reaches each one leaves.
- A result card on top counts the candidate sites.
- Site length, gaps and the quality filters fold away under More settings.
- Reset to starting values replaces the starting-values menu.
- The map's colors are switched in its legend: BHR, ER or Sites.
- Sites draws candidate sites in orange and every other reach in one gray.
- One gray for reaches with no data, and a Color by label on the legend.
- Reaches stand out more: a white edge, and wider lines zoomed in.
- A cleaner reach popup: the cross-section and its details in two tabs.
- Back and Next stay in view on small screens.
- Reaches show only zoomed in, and a study area's from a wider view.
- No watershed names on hover before reaches can be seen.
- The map's hint sits centered under the step tabs.
- The zoom buttons sit under the layers button, with the other map controls.

## v0.2.1 (2026-11-19)
- USGS Imagery basemap works again: USGS retired its imagery-with-labels service.

## v0.2.0 (2026-11-19)
- A reach's popup shows its cross-sections beside the reach medians.
- Drag or type a section's bankfull depth and low-bank height.
- An edited reach is screened on its edited sections; map and sites follow.
- Pull any section from USGS 3DEP on request, as the grid sampled it.
- Edits are saved with the screening (file version 2) and marked in downloads.
- Faster hover on the map; watershed names in the cue; examples open Screen.
- Third-party notices: geotiff and its packages added, fonts' notice completed.

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-19)
- First beta of Step 1, Screen sites, on the national cross-section grid.
- Pick watersheds, everything upstream of a point, or a box as the study area.
- Reaches of about 1,000 ft colored by bank height ratio or entrenchment ratio.
- Criteria with a funnel that shows how each one narrows the reaches.
- Candidate sites along each main stem, ranked, kept or dropped, with notes.
- Shortlist as CSV and GeoJSON, every reach as CSV, and a printable summary.
- Save and Open a screening; Steps 2 to 4 shown as planned.
